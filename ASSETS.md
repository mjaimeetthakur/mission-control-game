# Visual credits

The Earth globe uses **Blue Marble Next Generation**, the May 2004 land, ocean, topography and bathymetry composite from NASA. The bundled 2,048 × 1,024 JPEG comes from NASA WorldWind. The viewer applies simulated lighting and rotation. [Original image](https://github.com/NASAWorldWind/WebWorldWind/blob/develop/images/BMNG_world.topo.bathy.200405.3.2048x1024.jpg) · [NASA WorldWind project and license](https://github.com/NASAWorldWind/WebWorldWind) · [NASA imagery guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)

The Moon, Mars, fictional asteroid, orbital paths, spacecraft modules and launch vehicles are original illustrative models generated in the game. Interface icons use Lucide under the ISC license. The game is an independent educational project.
